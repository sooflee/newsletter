Undercurrent

Britain promised to make employers say what they pay; four of 27 EU states met the same rule's deadline.

On November 5 Andy Burnham's government said it would legislate to make salary information mandatory in British job adverts, citing a consultation in which 94 percent of respondents backed the requirement, including 93 percent of businesses. Ministers have not set the scope, the company-size threshold, the enforcement mechanism or a date; the promise is only that legislation comes 'as soon as possible'. The comparison is the point. The same obligation already exists in EU law, and its June 7 2026 transposition deadline passed with only Slovakia, Italy, Lithuania and Malta in force. Britain's own figures make the case: 41 percent of employers always publish a range, 18 percent a specific salary.

What the readings mean

Volatility measures how much the market is expected to move in the near term compared with the longer term, so a lower reading means less immediate stress. The yield curve is the gap between long-term and short-term government borrowing rates, and a steep curve usually points to expected growth rather than recession. When crypto is described as risk-off, investors are stepping back from the most speculative assets, which often serves as an early note of caution beneath a calm market.

The wildcard · Privacy and digital rights

Claude Code's 'don't ask again' wrote 1,379 real secrets into 1,122 public GitHub files.

ApiiroA security vendor that scanned public GitHub repositories for secrets left by coding agents between November 1 and 3, 2026. found 2,539 real secrets in 1,994 public GitHub repositories, most of them in files that record what coding agents were asked to run. The clearest case is Claude Code's own permission file: of 104,185 public copies of .claude/settings.local.jsonClaude Code's local permission file, meant to stay on the developer's machine. When it is committed to a public repository, every approved command, including any password in it, becomes public., 1,122 held a real secret, 1,379 in all, 636 provider keys and 743 passwords or tokens for public hosts, among them a database vendor's own cloud key with manage access and the owner password of a medical-records app's database. Approving a command with 'don't ask again' saves the whole command, secret included, to a file meant to stay on the laptop. Apiiro says a commit Claude co-authored changed the file in 539 of the 1,105 repositories with a real secret that it traced.
