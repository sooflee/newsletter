The bank, the spy agency and Apple's services broke inside 48 hours, and none of it was a hack.

On Monday November 16 thousands of customers reported trouble reaching accounts at Chase and other US banks or moving money through Zelle; Chase blamed 'a software bug in an on-premise hardware system' and said it was 'not cyber-related'. Marco Rubio said there was 'zero' evidence the incidents were linked or caused by a malign actor.

The ICJ found Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya, the first such judgment against a state.

On November 19 the International Court of JusticeThe UN's principal judicial organ, based in The Hague. It hears disputes between states, not cases against individuals. found Myanmar committed genocide against the RohingyaA mostly Muslim ethnic minority in Myanmar. About 740,000 fled to Bangladesh after the military's 2017 clearance operations in Rakhine State. during the 2017 clearance operations in northern Rakhine StateA state on Myanmar's western coast, bordering Bangladesh, where the 2017 military operations against the Rohingya took place., the first time the court has held a state responsible for genocide. The finding carried 9 votes to 6; the court said the evidence supported an estimate of 10,000 killed and ordered Myanmar to stop the violations, legislate, investigate and prosecute, and allow safe return, with reparations deferred to a later phase. Myanmar's foreign ministry called the judgment 'a great disappointment' the next day. The same week, Trump, Putin and Xi met in Shenzhen and said the ICC must not abuse its jurisdiction. Tajikistan deposited its withdrawal from the Rome StatuteThe 1998 treaty that established the International Criminal Court. A state party can withdraw with one year's notice under Article 127..

Key factThe court rejected the charge that Myanmar directly and publicly incited genocide, by 14 votes to 1, and left the amount and modalities of reparations to a later phase, with Myanmar to report within two years.

What the readings mean

Volatility measures how much the market is expected to move in the near term compared with the longer term, so a lower reading means less immediate stress. The yield curve is the gap between long-term and short-term government borrowing rates, and a steep curve usually points to expected growth rather than recession. When crypto is described as risk-off, investors are stepping back from the most speculative assets, which often serves as an early note of caution beneath a calm market.

The wildcard · Science & frontier: formal verification

Levent Alpöge's Claude-assisted proof of the Riemann Hypothesis is certified correct by the Lean community in a day.

Levent Alpöge posted a LeanA proof assistant that checks every step of a mathematical argument by computer, so a formalised theorem depends on the kernel rather than a referee's reading. library of 17 million lines on November 20 that he says, working with ClaudeAnthropic's family of AI models. Alpöge says he worked with Claude on the proof and that Claude outpaced him near the finish., proves the Riemann HypothesisA conjecture from 1859 about the distribution of prime numbers. A proof would sharpen the error term in the prime number theorem and settle a central question in number theory. and extends to the Generalized Riemann HypothesisAn extension of the Riemann Hypothesis from the Riemann zeta function to other L-functions, including Dirichlet L-functions. It implies the Riemann Hypothesis but is a stronger claim.. The Lean FROThe Lean Focused Research Organization and the community library Mathlib. Their joint statement certified that Alpöge's library compiles and proves the same statement as Mathlib's Riemann hypothesis. and the Mathlib Initiative certified the next day that the library proves Mathlib's statement of the Riemann Hypothesis, after the code compiled for dozens of people and independent checkers confirmed the exported proof. Terence Tao described it as an enormous Lean-only proof of the Generalized Riemann Hypothesis generated with an internal AI model. Ravi Vakil had meanwhile announced a competing proposed human proof of the Generalized Riemann Hypothesis by Alessandro Masullo, written without AI, and reported that several people had already found problems in it.
