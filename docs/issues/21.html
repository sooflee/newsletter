What the readings mean

Volatility measures how much the market is expected to move in the near term compared with the longer term, so a lower reading means less immediate stress. The yield curve is the gap between long-term and short-term government borrowing rates, and a steep curve usually points to expected growth rather than recession. When crypto is described as risk-off, investors are stepping back from the most speculative assets, which often serves as an early note of caution beneath a calm market.

The wildcard · Crypto and fintech

TraderTraitor took $6.3 billion from Binance in twenty minutes, and stablecoin issuers froze $2.67 billion.

North Korea's TraderTraitorA North Korean state-backed hacking unit, part of the Lazarus Group, blamed for major cryptocurrency thefts. unit took about $6.3 billion from Binance on October 29, the largest crypto theft on record. Transfers began around 4pm ET and most of the money left within twenty minutes: five XRP Ledger wallets were emptied between 20:05 and 20:09 UTC, 18,270 BTC moved at 20:20, and on Ethereum the attacker used 50 pre-signed EIP-7702An Ethereum upgrade that lets an account delegate control to a smart contract, which the Binance attacker used with pre-signed authorizations. authorizations to turn a Binance hot wallet into a sweeper it controlled. TRM puts the total at $6.36 billion at theft prices across ten chains and blames Binance's hot-wallet signing system. TetherThe issuers of USDT and USDC, the two largest dollar stablecoins, which can freeze tokens on their networks., Circle, Ripple and World Liberty Financial froze about $2.67 billion of stablecoins, and Arbitrum's security council froze $30 million more; the attacker still holds roughly $2.4 billion in bitcoin and ether. Binance suspended withdrawals and reopened them on November 1.

The CFTC uses the breach to argue for its exchange rules (CNBC, October 30)
Chairman Michael Selig told CNBC the breach 'proves our exact point' about the crypto rules the agency floated on October 5, an advance notice that would offer offshore exchanges an optional federal registration path. It predates the hack by more than three weeks.

Discord will put Discoverable Server messages into web search (Discord, October 27)
From November 16 the 63,000 Discoverable Servers get public pages, and from mid-December messages in public channels can appear in search results, switched on by default outside the EU, UK, Canada, Brazil, Australia and South Korea.

Lenovo's 'encrypted' fingerprint USB was a plain SD card (Tom's Hardware, updated November 3)
Researcher Xusheng Li found last year that the ThinkPlus FU100's fingerprint reader only switched on the USB connection, leaving files unencrypted on a microSD card; Lenovo says the mainland-China-only drives were remediated and three CVEs were published in January.
